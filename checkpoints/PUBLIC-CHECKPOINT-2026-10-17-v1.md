### What this work demonstrates

- **AI workflow architecture:** decompose a business process into actors, tools, durable state, evidence, human decisions and external consequences.
- **Agent reliability:** investigate failure paths, ambiguous outcomes, retries, missing data and cross-service assumptions.
- **Authority and provenance:** distinguish who supplied information, who may act, what was approved and which evidence supports a claim.
- **Evaluation design:** challenge the tests and fixtures as well as the implementation; keep reported results separate from independently reproduced results.
- **Operational interfaces:** make uncertainty and source status understandable to the person using the system.
- **Engineering judgment:** preserve failed assumptions and unresolved findings so a later revision cannot quietly inherit unearned confidence.
